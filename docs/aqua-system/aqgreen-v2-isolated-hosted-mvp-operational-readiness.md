## 3. Test money and financial safety

- All displayed ZAR amounts, payment IDs, sales quantities and ledgers are
  **simulated records, not earnings, debt, settlement, insurance commencement or
  payable obligations**. No copying into business accounts or member statements.
- No real Yoco payments, Bird deliveries, customer data, commerce or payouts.
  Disable external deliveries and financial workers; no production secrets,
  production data clone, shared business database or real provider callback path.
  Labels supplement technical isolation; they cannot establish it.
- Weekly rule remains **5/5/5**, separately per named product. 5/5/4 and 15/0/0
  are Confirmed + NotMet when supported; missing/conflicting evidence is Held,
  not an invented zero or failed threshold. No substitution or carry-forward.
- **Sales HeldForEvidence** is unresolved eligibility and can block the Tenant/week
  calculation. **Financial Held** is a calculated positive amount with an evidenced
  financial hold. **Earned/unreleased** is not automatically financially Held.
  Inspect actual status/reason and null release/payment times, not just a banner.
- The original positive **R150 Held** scenario is carried-forward evidence; the
  derivative's **R400 Earned/unreleased** example does not replace it. Technical
  handoff must supply an accessible controlled positive-held fixture/result for
  the pilot, clearly identified as synthetic. No backdating newly joined members
  or rewriting finalized history to produce it.
- Release and RecordPayment must remain backend-denied even for permissioned
  financial actors. No optional “test payout”, manual amount entry or temporary
  bypass. Current public calculation targets the latest closed week; missing
  eligible fixture evidence means pause that demonstration, not historical repair.

## 4. Administrative evidence and duplicate prevention

Adopt the [administrative playbook §§2–4](aqgreen-v2-mvp-administrative-operations-playbook.md)
using a small owner-approved **synthetic evidence pack** and restricted shared
register, not real invoices or customer records. No new platform is needed.

1. **Identify:** record case ID, synthetic Tenant/Area/participation, closed week,
   evidence references, supported sale date/product/units, reason/basis and actor/time.
   Map invoice/receipt/renamed copies to one underlying issuer + sale + line key.
2. **Search and reserve:** writer checks reservations and finalized allocations
   across members and retained weeks in that Tenant before confirmation. Reserve
   counted units once; supported splits must never exceed source totals. If stable
   identity or authorized cross-Area clearance is unavailable, hold/escalate.
3. **Finalize and read back:** reviewer submits; writer links immutable app decision
   ID and closes the reservation once. **Confirmed + NotMet consumes its counted
   units**. Unknown submit outcome retains reservation pending readback; no blind
   retry, erasure or reuse next week.
4. **Reconcile and preserve:** reconcile register totals to confirmed quantities;
   append disputes/corrections without rewriting final allocations. Companion log
   carries hold reason, confirmation basis and next action where the app does not.
   Opaque app references must point to that restricted record.

The app does **not** enforce global sale-line deduplication. The accepted evidence
includes a single-writer synthetic exercise, not proof of multi-administrator
discipline. Register custodianship never grants extra Tenant/Area record access.
Late or finalized disputes remain logged/held for owner disposition, not a real
business remedy or general correction/backfill programme.

## 5. Consent and written-observation rules

**Owner rule: no recordings.** No audio, video, screenshots, screen recordings,
browser traces/HAR or session replay to record participants; disable meeting,
browser and hosting analytics capture accordingly. Do not substitute automatic
activity capture for written notes. Minimal system audit facts needed for test
decisions are distinct from participant recordings; logs must exclude secrets,
request bodies, one-time links and identifying content.

- Before human tasks, explain the isolated purpose, voluntary participation,
  simulated data/money and right to stop. Obtain separate informed agreement
  **before any de-identified written observations**. Owner permission is not consent.
  Declining notes must not generate observation evidence or an inferred result.
- Tell each participant the **specific readers, custodian, restricted location,
  retention period/deletion date and withdrawal method** approved under §7.
  Until these are decided and individual consent obtained, collect no observations.
- Keep only session/role alias, task, expected/actual result, brief paraphrased
  misunderstanding, assistance and next action. No names, contact details, personal
  stories, real financial information, identifying quotations or credential/token
  material. Role-labelled notes may still be identifiable; keep them restricted.
- Only owner-designated readers receive access. Custodian keeps notes and minimal
  consent entries outside Git, public issues, chat transcripts and public links;
  no name-to-alias map is required. Synthetic registers and technical evidence
  have their own approved access/retention, not automatic observation access.
- On withdrawal, stop collecting immediately. Participant can request deletion by
  session/role alias through the agreed route; custodian removes attributable notes
  and copies under the agreed policy, recording only a non-identifying deletion
  completion. Delete at the approved deadline, including exports/backups under
  custodian control; explain any technical deletion delay before consent, not afterward.

## 6. Shutdown and cleanup ownership

- **Immediate suspension:** lead requests stop; technical operator blocks participant
  ingress and stops API/frontend and all pilot background processes, prevents
  automatic restart and confirms operations are no longer reachable. Disabling
  accounts alone is insufficient if existing sessions/tokens remain usable.
- **Access removal:** custodian disables pilot identities and revokes supported
  sessions; operator closes verification helpers, invitations/access routes and
  temporary access channels. Do not assume invitation expiry—the accepted model
  has no expiry field. Stopped/deleted isolated state must make old links unusable.
- **Evidence first:** custodian preserves only the agreed sanitized evidence and
  consented observations until their separate retention deadlines. No database
  dump, secret store or participant trace is an evidence substitute.
- **Pilot-only cleanup:** operator uses an exact resource inventory to remove only
  this environment's disposable database/volumes, caches, logs, synthetic outbox,
  temporary secrets and hosting resources under owner-approved cleanup authority.
  Keep accepted source archives/manifests and approved retained evidence. No broad
  shared-resource deletion or deletion of existing business records.
- **Closure:** lead records access stopped, resource removal, retained-evidence
  custodian/deletion date and any unresolved item. Restart requires owner approval
  and fresh preflight. This assignment performs none of these hosted operations.
