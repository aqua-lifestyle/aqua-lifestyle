# AQGreen V2 isolated discovery and task scripts

**PROPOSED research protocol; not business authority or interview findings.**
No outreach, real payment, publication or commercial activation is authorized by
this document. Use consented adults, test identities and test money in disposable
infrastructure; keep de-identified observations separate from test-run evidence.

## Discovery, 15–20 minutes, before showing the product

Opening: “We are researching how people buy or sell these goods/services and
manage membership tasks. This is not an earnings offer. You can decline any
question or stop. May we take de-identified notes?”

- “Tell me about the last time you bought or sold a product like the one on the
  owner-approved AQGreen SKU card. What led to that transaction?”
- “What did you use before? What did it cost, and what happened afterwards?”
- “Tell me about a recent repeat purchase, return or unsold item.”
- “How did you find the buyer/seller? Was the buyer also a programme member?”
- “Walk me through how you kept evidence of payment, delivery and any refund.”
- “Tell me about the last membership application or approval you handled. Where
  did you get stuck, and what did you do?”
- For operators: “Walk me through the most recent disputed sales claim. What
  evidence did you have, what was missing, and how was it resolved?”
- “What have I missed? Who has a different experience?”

Ask for voluntary redacted examples, never credentials or other people's personal
records. Probe specific past events; avoid “Would you join?”, “Do you like it?”,
income promises, presenting 5/5/5 as easy, and treating praise as purchase intent.
Sample buyers who do not recruit, dissatisfied/declining people and operators;
do not select only enthusiastic sponsors.
Obtain an owner-approved SKU/use card before any product-value observation; current
spray identity and intended use remain unresolved. Generic workflow observations
may proceed without making claims about product demand.

## Observed tasks, 20–30 minutes, after discovery

Opening: “This is a disposable test-money environment. No real payment or payout
will occur. Please work as you normally would and say what you think is happening.
We are testing the system. You may stop.” Give each actor only their own login.
Use synthetic evidence documents conspicuously marked TEST.

| Actor | Neutral task prompt | Observer checks (do not read as hints) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorized root/sponsor | “Invite someone to this programme.” | Finds invitation; understands invitation is not activation or earnings. |
| First-time member | “You received this invitation. Decide what to do next and complete the test joining task if you choose.” | Sponsor/programme/price understanding; own identity; no developer edits. |
| Member | “The test checkout has returned. Tell me your current status and what you can do next.” | Does not equate return with verified payment or payment with approval. |
| Member | “Leave and return later. Find your application and explain its status.” | Pending status survives refresh, direct URL and new tab; next action discoverable. |
| Assigned Area admin | “Find applications awaiting your decision and handle this one using the information available.” | Correct Area; authority; pending → Active plus one placement; rejects other Area. |
| Member | “Find your progress and tell me what it says about your activity and earnings.” | Separates direct recruits, placement, structural level, sales and unreleased money. |
| Sales reviewer | “Review this week's evidence and record your decision.” | 5/5/5 independently; missing/duplicate/refunded evidence; review time/workload. |
| Commission operator | “Prepare the test week's calculation and explain the result.” | Versions/components/evidence; retry; no release or payout action. |
| Member | “Explain the amount shown and whether you can receive it now.” | Test money; earnings not paid; no invented eligibility or promised income. |

Record task completion, elapsed time, help requests, abandonment, wrong turns,
status interpretation and severity of errors. Delay assistance; when provided,
record it and distinguish assisted from independent completion. Do not enter
production data, conceal test status or manufacture sales.

## Evidence and decisions

Pre-register a small diagnostic sample (PROPOSED: five prospective members,
including non-recruiting buyers, and two administrators/reviewers). This sample
can reveal obstacles; it cannot estimate population demand or economic viability.
Seek recurring past purchase/use and actual observed understanding. No automatic
pass rate is approved here. Any identity/authorization violation, real-money
exposure or misleading payout claim stops the pilot immediately.

Observation template: pseudonymous actor, consent, date, task, observed actions,
redacted quote, outcome, assistance, evidence type, competing explanation, next
experiment. Record refusals and contrary evidence. Keep automated/TRX results in
a different register. Current actual-user register: **NOT RUN; no findings**.

Economic evidence template (currently UNKNOWN): product and period; independent
customer orders; net paid receipts after refunds/VAT; delivered quantities; unit
costs; fulfilment/support/acquisition costs; all commission obligations allocated
without counting one sale's margin repeatedly across ancestors; joining-fee
inflows shown separately; source-of-funds reconciliation. Calculate:
`commercial surplus = net external-customer receipts - product/fulfilment costs
- operating costs - all compensation liabilities`. Test zero-new-joiner,
lower-sales, returns and repeat-purchase scenarios. Do not fill missing costs
with zero or treat test ledger amounts as sales receipts.

## Critical use of methods

- [Ries](https://theleanstartup.com/principles): use explicit experiments and
  learning; test passes establish mechanics, not product-market fit.
- [Fitzpatrick](https://www.momtestbook.com/): ask about recent behaviour and
  alternatives; interviews remain susceptible to selection and recall bias.
- [Blank](https://steveblank.com/2009/11/30/customer-development-is-not-a-focus-group/):
  test customer/business hypotheses with customers; founders' beliefs are not facts.
- [Maurya](https://ashmaurya.com/books): expose desirability, feasibility and
  viability risks; a completed canvas does not validate them.
- [Torres](https://www.producttalk.org/getting-started-with-discovery/): establish
  repeat consented discovery tied to member outcomes; frequency alone proves nothing.
- [Savoia](https://www.pretotyping.org/methodology.html): test interest with a
  clearly disclosed low-cost task or sample before expansion; simulated payment
  and task compliance are not purchase demand. No deceptive fake-money offer.
- [Singer](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/1.2-chapter-03): bound scope to a complete
  slice and reuse existing code; time appetite cannot waive financial safeguards.
