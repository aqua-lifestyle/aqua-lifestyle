## 6. Economics, costs and obligations

### 6.1 Minimal model with known rule inputs

For period `t`, distinguish **company** economics from seller and whole-chain
economics. Where Aqua is a wholesaler, use its wholesale receipts and its costs;
seller retail turnover and retained markup belong in a separate seller account.
Where Aqua is principal merchant, use its net retail receipts. Never count both
wholesale and final retail turnover as consolidated external revenue.

```text
C_t = 150*a1_t + 400*a2_t + 1650*a3_t
  aL = participants at exactly that payable level under the period's rules
  Count newly earned and applicable held obligations, not just cash released.

G_t = company product receipts net of taxes/discounts/returns
      - company product, packaging, fulfillment and variable selling/payment costs
S_t = collectible legitimate membership-service receipts
      - costs of the goods/services/benefits included in those subscriptions

Recurring operating result = G_t + S_t - C_t - operating/review/support costs
                            - other benefit costs not already included
                            - appropriate loss/claims provisions

Cash closing = cash opening + actual settled inflows - actual outflows
               (separately schedule accrued commissions, returns and benefits)
```

Every missing amount remains **UNKNOWN**. No net result can currently be calculated.
Use actual calendar cycles for a cash forecast; `52/12` is only an annualized
illustration. Joining receipts `1200*newJoiners` are one-off gross receipts, not
automatically profit: joining fulfillment, inclusion, tax and refunds consume
them. A no-new-joiner scenario sets that inflow to zero. Savings principal and loan
principal are not sales revenue or an assumed commission backstop.

### 6.2 Network-wide obligation example

**INFERENCE from confirmed components; synthetic structure, not actual population.**
A complete three-depth tree has 156 participants: root + 5 + 25 + 125. Suppose
all relevant occupants qualify structurally and all 31 candidates meet their own
weekly sales gate, with no payout holds:

| Exact level | Members | Weekly amount/member | Weekly total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Level 3 root | 1 | R1,650 | R1,650 |
| Level 2 | 5 | R400 | R2,000 |
| Level 1 | 25 | R150 | R3,750 |
| No completed level | 125 | No structural commission in this example | R0 |
| **Total** | **156** | | **R7,400** |

The same occupants contribute to several ancestors' structures. All recipients'
obligations must be summed; do not analyze only the root or repeatedly allocate
the same sale margin. This is not a rule paying everyone at Level 3.

The quota requires sales by a commission candidate; it does **not** establish that
all 156 participants sell. For a purely retail-funded illustration, let `m` be
company contribution from one distinct 5/5/5 basket **after product/fulfillment/
variable costs but before fixed operations, benefits and commissions**:

| ASSUMED `m`, not a price or verified margin | Residual with 31 selling candidates | Residual if all 156 independently sell |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R100 | −R4,300/week | R8,200/week |
| R250 | R350/week | R31,600/week |
| R500 | R8,100/week | R70,600/week |

Residual is `sellers*m − R7400`, before remaining costs. Retail commission-only
break-even basket contribution is R238.71 for 31 sellers or R47.44 for 156 sellers.
Neither scenario is demand evidence; non-candidates' sales cannot be assumed.
Each threshold basket contains 15 units, including **30 litres in the 1L/5L
categories plus five sprays**. Exactly 5/5/5 for 52 weeks is 260 units/category,
780 total units. Seller inventory, demand and transport consequences matter.

**Subscription stress illustration:** if all 156 participants pay 12 monthly
R600 obligations, gross monthly receipts are R93,600, equivalent to R21,600/week
over 52 weeks. This exceeds the example R7,400 commission total *before* benefit,
service, tax and operating costs. Thus the known rates alone do not prove
insolvency. They also do not prove independent retail sustainability: dues paid
to pursue network rewards can reflect recruitment-derived economic dependence.
Subscription renewal without rewards and delivered value require separate proof.

Stress the eventual costed model with zero joining inflow, lower voluntary repeat
sales, missed monthly collections, higher delivery costs, refunds and delayed
settlement. Re-evaluate each week's actual eligibility rather than assume that
commissions scale proportionally with sales. A hold delays release; it must not
be assumed to erase a liability. Inclusion costs may also concern fully paid
pending or rejected participants, not just Active members. Keep those populations
and commitments visible when testing cash sufficiency.

As a conditional upper-bound check, each non-root occupant can contribute at
most R30 + R10 + R10 = R50/week across its three immediate applicable placement
ancestors under the stated three-depth components. Therefore total structural
commissions are at most `50*(N−numberOfRoots)` before gates; actual incomplete
levels lower the total. This bound explains why extrapolating R1,650 to every
member is wrong. It is not funding evidence or legal clearance.

## 7. Recruitment versus genuine retail dependence

**What is established:** structure is participant-derived; V2 adds an own-sales
gate but retains fixed level components. Direct recruitment attribution is
separate from placement; spillover can advance an ancestor without transferring
sponsor credit. Existing structure can persist without further recruitment.

**What is not established:** the origin of funds actually supporting compensation,
whether customers buy for consumption without earning incentives, or whether
promotion emphasizes products/services rather than recruitment/income.

The decisive counterfactual is: **with no new joiners, can existing customers
voluntarily continue buying useful products/services and support all continuing
obligations at realistic costs?** Track independent external net receipts,
recurring member service value, seller stock sell-through, returns, repeat use and
all commissions. Non-member sales are a strong evidence category, not a statutory
percentage safe harbor. Genuine member consumption should be identified
separately rather than automatically called either retail independence or fraud.

**Quota distortion risk (INFERENCE):** 5/5/5 can encourage valuable selling, but
could also motivate a person to buy stock, finance friendly buyers, exchange
reciprocal purchases or conceal refunds to unlock a fixed payout. Receipt
existence alone cannot distinguish those cases. No such behavior has been
observed by this research. High turnover generated solely to preserve eligibility
is not proof of durable product demand.

## 12. Recommendation and evidence that changes it

### CHANGE — proposed

Continue the narrow isolated engineering slice as a learning tool. Change its
commercial success definition to **specified useful products + standalone
membership value + traceable legitimate recurring contribution + protected,
informed members**. A functioning network/commission journey is necessary for the
chosen design but cannot validate that business model.

**Evidence that would support CONTINUE toward a separately authorized commercial
pilot:** current deliverable/claims authority; independent voluntary use and repeat
demand without earning incentives; acceptable seller outcomes; all commissions and
benefits funded at realistic costs without new-joiner receipts and with stress
liquidity; demonstrated comprehension; and fact-specific South African advice with
required remedies/authorizations in place. None is supplied by synthetic E2E.

**Evidence that would require further CHANGE:** useful goods but insufficient
membership value, quota pressure, negative seller outcomes, unavailable insurer
benefit, weak margins or misleading promotion. Any revised fee, formula, threshold
or benefit requires owner authorization and versioned treatment; no policy change
is made here.

**Evidence that would support STOP the proposed commercial model:** durable demand
exists only with recruiting/income incentives; obligations require continuous new
joining money or use of entrusted savings; repeat commerce remains uneconomic at
credible prices/costs; material benefit/health claims cannot be supported; or
qualified advice identifies prohibited conduct that the owner will not remedy.
Missing evidence today warrants withholding commercialization, not declaring
AQGreen unlawful or commercially doomed.
