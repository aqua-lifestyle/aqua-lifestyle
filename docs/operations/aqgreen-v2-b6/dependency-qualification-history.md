# AQGreen V2 B6 dependency-qualification history

This record preserves dependency evidence that remains relevant after the candidate
architecture changed from fresh unlocked replay to native NuGet locked replay. It is
historical qualification evidence, not current operational package authority. The
detailed retained transcripts and frozen disposable artifacts remain the source for
attempt-specific claims; this summary does not replace them.

## Evidence boundary

The dependency identities remain distinct:

- raw `.nupkg` bytes and raw SHA-256/SHA-512;
- NuGet canonical content identity;
- package/nuspec/provenance identity;
- installed global-packages tree identity;
- selected graph identity.

Acquired does not mean selected or resolution-authorized. A generated lock is not a
trusted lock, and a resolved graph is not an authorized graph.

## Attempts 1–12

Attempts 1–12 remain preserved as the prior B6 acquisition, confinement, graph,
package-identity, provenance, cache/source-visibility, and operational-runner
investigation record. Later architecture does not retroactively promote, erase, or
reinterpret their evidence states.

## Attempt 13 — selected graph is not an unlocked resolver universe

An independently fresh unlocked restore with only canonical G197 exited zero but
emitted 59 NU1603 warnings: 26 unique warning edges requested 19 unique missing
lower-bound ID/version pairs. Those 19 pairs became S19/L1, the first observed
ResolverSupport layer.

Established conclusion:

> G197 is insufficient for warning-free fresh unlocked resolution under the tested
> conditions.

## Attempt 14 — raw archive and NuGet canonical hashes differ

Attempt 14 failed before B1 because its helper incorrectly required the metadata
`contentHash` and assets `sha512` to equal the raw archive SHA-512. Signed packages
can preserve NuGet canonical content identity while their raw signed archive bytes
differ. The corrected contract retains raw archive identity and NuGet canonical
content identity as separate validations.

## Attempt 15 — ZIP paths and extracted paths differ

Attempt 15 failed before B1 because it compared raw ZIP entry names directly with
NuGet-installed filesystem paths. NuGet extraction applies its own validated path
normalisation, including percent decoding. For example, the Microsoft.CSharp entry
containing `%2B` materializes with `+`. Reference extraction must use the pinned
NuGet semantics, detect normalized collisions/traversal, and compare actual installed
paths rather than reconstructing them from raw ZIP names.

## Attempt 16 — ResolverSupport is recursively incomplete

Attempt 16 content-bound all 238 acquired packages and established runtime
populations A=238, G=197, A-G=41, S19=19, and S22=22. Its B1 run 1 used G+S19 in a
fresh cache with network absent and only that local resolver source. Restore exited
zero but emitted 39 NU1603 warnings, including 17 unique support-of-support edges
and 16 new lower-bound targets inside S22/A-G. The run failed closed before graph
validation.

Established classifications:

- R0 warning-free sufficiency: **REJECTED for the current graph/test conditions**.
- R0 selection preservation: **UNTESTED**.
- R0 minimality: **UNTESTED**.
- S19/L1 and L2 remain historical diagnostic layers, not operational authority.

## Native locked-mode diagnostic

The later unprivileged disposable prototype generated byte-identical per-project
native `packages.lock.json` files twice. Their union was exact G197 with canonical
SHA-256 `a32d7081f7c687c9ece1511122dd772ac17e7cfc682943e5706ac0caef6fc4a0`.
A selected-only fresh-cache `dotnet restore --locked-mode` succeeded with zero
warnings, NU1603, or errors, left the locks unchanged, and generated assets whose
union was exact G197. S19/L1, L2, and all other A-G residue were absent. Declaration,
package-absence, tested lock-tamper, and tested package-content mutations failed
closed. A following `dotnet build --no-restore` succeeded; missing assets produced
NETSDK1004 without a silent restore.

Established distinction:

> G197 insufficient for unlocked resolution does not imply G197 insufficient for
> locked materialization.

## Candidate architecture consequence

The candidate operational model is B1-Q resolution qualification, separate approval
of exact native lockfiles and canonical G, A-L routine locked acquisition of that
already-approved material, B1-R selected-only locked reproduction, then B2
selected-only `--no-restore` build. A-L obtains a dependency decision but does not
make one; normal operation therefore does not turn B1-Q into recurring dynamic
resolution. Recursive ResolverSupport promotion, warning closure, support
minimization, and support non-selection tests are no longer active operational
dependencies. They may be retired only after the replacement is reviewed and
operationally qualified; their failed and successful experiments remain evidence.
