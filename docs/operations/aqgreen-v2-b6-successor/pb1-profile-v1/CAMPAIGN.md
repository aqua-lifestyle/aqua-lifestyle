## Three finite approvals

1. **Owner:** approve the exact proposal-manifest identity and C0 fixture-only scope,
   including the new parent-observer/deadline/enforcer behavior. Separately authorize
   only the bounded controller implementation after that selection; no refusal
   removal, broader B6 implementation or production capability follows.
2. **Operator/custodian:** identify the approved disposable platform, setup principal
   and exact namespace identity/custody route. Actual privileged test setup requires
   explicit authority; no new production service, credential or deployment is needed.
3. **Independent examiner:** accept the actual controller/observer/release/dependency/
   kernel/ABI/custody inputs and C05 probe before executing the campaign. A proposal
   or future unbuilt executable cannot be marked selected/qualified by a placeholder.
   The recording role's exact closed wire/native-layout/decoder contract must be
   selected/reviewed before its implementation, not inferred from this fact list.

Approval of behavior/roles and the required exact observation contract can precede

self-qualification/hash cycle. Each change in selected bytes/profile requires a
